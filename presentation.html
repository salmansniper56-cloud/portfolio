Live Product · beingsalmanbutt.app
Pocket AI
Founder & Solo Developer — Personal Product

Inspired by Pocket Pal AI, Pocket AI is a privacy-first AI assistant that runs large language models entirely on-device — no cloud, no data leaks. Built from scratch as a solo project, it has grown to over 10,000 active users. Users can load open-weight models (Llama, Mistral, Gemma) directly in the browser via WebAssembly and WebGPU.
